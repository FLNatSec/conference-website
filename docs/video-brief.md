# Homepage hero video — production brief

The homepage hero is a short, silent, looping montage graded to the brand's **navy → ice duotone**. It mixes:

- **Real public-domain footage** for anything factual or geographic (launches, Florida from orbit). Source: NASA. Recorded in `docs/asset-sources.md`.
- **AI-generated atmospheric b-roll** for generic mood shots (coastline, sea, sky), generated by the organizers from the prompts below.

**Current state:** a ~29 s loop of real U.S. Government footage. It opens with NASA's Artemis I launch (pad → ignition → ascent), then DVIDS clips: USCGC *Eagle* under sail, a bomber formation over the coast, and sunset from a ship's bridge. Unused DVIDS candidates reviewed on 2026-10-05: Blue Angels at sunset, CH-53 boat lift, Atlas launch, Starship-area launch, Army helicopters, F-22 demo, missile test, LCS flight deck. They were skipped for tone (too tactical or weapons-forward) or quality.

### The university shot (UF)

A shot that clearly *shows UF* — campus landmarks, labs, students — should be **real UF footage, used with UF's permission**, not AI:

- AI can't depict a real campus accurately. It invents buildings and signage, and a fake-looking "UF" would undercut credibility.
- UF's name, marks, and landmarks are controlled by the university. Ask UF Strategic Communications (or the Herbert Wertheim College of Engineering / EII communications team) for b-roll. Request a 5–10 s clip at 1080p or larger, no people in close-up, no visible logos or wordmarks unless UF approves their use.
- In the loop, label it by place (`GAINESVILLE · 29.65°N 82.32°W`), not as a UF endorsement. Keep it one shot among several so the summit doesn't read as UF-owned. Future hosts can swap in their own campus shot.

If UF footage isn't available, use prompt 4 (generic research lab), labeled "Research".

## Hard rules for every AI shot

These protect credibility with government and military audiences, and keep usage rights clean.

- **No** military hardware, weapons, aircraft, warships, or vehicles that read as military.
- **No** insignia, seals, flags, hull numbers, logos, signage, or any text.
- **No** identifiable people or faces.
- **No** depictions of real installations, real events, or recognizable landmarks presented as real places.
- **No** fake "Florida" geography. If a shot must be geographically true (e.g., the peninsula from orbit), use real NASA imagery instead.
- **Avoid** HUD overlays, glowing UI, drones, and "sci-fi" looks.
- **Rights:** generate on a paid plan whose terms grant commercial use of outputs. Record the tool, plan, prompt, and date in `docs/asset-sources.md`.

## Look

- Blue hour or night; deep navy shadows, cool highlights. (The duotone grade is applied in the edit, so color accuracy doesn't matter — **contrast and composition do**.)
- Slow, steady drone-style moves: drift, push, or slow lateral track. No shake, no fast cuts, no speed ramps.
- 24 fps, 5–8 s per shot. Generate at **1920×1080 or larger, 16:9**. Keep the subject **right of center** with dark negative space on the left, where the title sits. Also keep it **crop-safe for a 9:16 vertical center-right crop** for phones.

## Shot prompts (paste into Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling, etc.)

**1. Coastline, pre-dawn**
> Slow aerial drone shot drifting forward over a long, low barrier island and calm ocean at pre-dawn blue hour, thin low clouds, faint city lights far on the horizon, soft surf lines, deep navy shadows, cool tones, cinematic, photorealistic, 24fps, no text, no people, no boats, no logos.

**2. Sea lane, wake from above**
> Top-down aerial shot slowly tracking a large generic cargo ship's long white wake across dark open ocean at dusk, ship partly out of frame at right, calm water texture, cinematic, photorealistic, 24fps, no text, no flags, no markings, no logos, no people.

**3. Port at night (optional)**
> Slow lateral aerial track past rows of shipping containers and tall cranes at a large seaport at night, sodium lights, light haze, long exposure feel, cinematic, photorealistic, 24fps, no text, no logos, no people, no flags.

**4. Research / talent (optional, abstract)**
> Slow push-in on a dark, modern university engineering lab at night, glowing equipment status lights out of focus, shallow depth of field, cinematic, photorealistic, 24fps, no people, no faces, no screens with readable text, no logos.

**Real-footage shots (sourced by the developer, not AI):** additional NASA launches and NASA Earth-observation night imagery of Florida.

Generate 2–3 takes per prompt and keep the calmest one. Reject any take with warped geometry, melting objects, readable pseudo-text, or flicker.

## Edit and encode recipe (ffmpeg)

The duotone maps grayscale luminance onto the brand tokens `ink` (#08111C) → `bone` (#EAF0F5):

```
DUO="format=gray,format=rgb24,curves=all='0/0 0.35/0.25 0.8/0.82 1/1',lutrgb=r='8+val*226/255':g='17+val*223/255':b='28+val*217/255'"
```

For each shot: `trim` → optional slow-motion (`setpts=(PTS-STARTPTS)/0.6`) → `crop` (16:9 desktop or 9:16 mobile) → `scale` (1920:1080 or 720:1280) → `fps=24` → fade in/out (shots dip to navy). Then `concat`, apply `$DUO`, add light grain (`noise=alls=6:allf=t`), and encode:

```
-an -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 27 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart
```

Targets: desktop ≤ ~5 MB, mobile ≤ ~2.5 MB. Extract a poster frame (`-ss 3 -frames:v 1 -q:v 3`) from each encode.

**Framing checks before publishing:**

- The subject stays right of center on desktop, so it's not behind the title.
- No frame is bright enough to wash out white text.
- The loop end cuts cleanly to the start.
